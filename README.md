# Sales Dashboard

### Microsoft Excel | Data Analysis | Data Visualization

## Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Sales Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel** to convert raw sales data into clear and actionable business insights.

The dashboard brings together sales, profit, customer, product, regional and monthly performance in a single view. Interactive filters allow users to analyze the data by **Year** and **Category**.

The primary objective was to make business performance easier to understand without going through large volumes of raw data.

## Business Problem

Raw sales data can make it difficult for a business to quickly understand performance and identify areas that require attention.

The key challenges addressed in this project were:

* Difficulty in identifying high and low-performing products
* Limited visibility into profit trends across different years
* Difficulty comparing customer contribution
* Lack of a clear geographical view of sales performance
* Difficulty identifying monthly sales trends
* Time-consuming manual analysis of large datasets
* No single interactive view for comparing different categories and years

---

## Solution

To address these problems, I developed an interactive Excel dashboard that provides a consolidated view of important sales and business metrics.

The dashboard allows users to:

* Analyze sales performance by product
* Track profit trends over time
* Compare yearly customer counts
* Identify high-performing states
* Analyze the contribution of top customers
* Monitor monthly sales trends
* Filter the entire dashboard using Year and Category slicers

This approach reduces the need for manual data exploration and makes important patterns easier to identify.

---

## Key Dashboard Components

### Sales by Product

Displays product-level sales performance and helps identify products with higher and lower sales contributions.

### Profit Gained Over Time

Shows how profit changes across different years and categories, helping understand the overall profitability trend.

### Customer Count

Provides a yearly comparison of customer count to understand changes in the customer base.

### Sales by State

Uses geographical visualization to compare sales performance across different states.

### Top 5 Customers Making Profit

Highlights the top customers based on their profit contribution and helps identify valuable customers.

### Monthly Sales

Shows monthly sales movement and helps identify periods of higher and lower sales activity.

### Interactive Slicers

The dashboard includes:

* **Year**
* **Category**

These slicers allow users to dynamically filter the dashboard and analyze specific business segments.
